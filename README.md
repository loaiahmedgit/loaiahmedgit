## About

I am a Computer Science student at Qatar University, graduating in December 2027, focused on building reliable software where AI, data, and product engineering meet. My strongest areas are Python, SQL, relational schema design, and turning complex product requirements into working systems across web and mobile.

My current work includes **NASQ**, a privacy-first workforce presence and continuity platform, and **Maseera**, a full-stack social platform backed by PostgreSQL and seven relational models.

I approach engineering as a complete product discipline: understand the user problem, design the data model, build the interface and services, test the critical paths, and measure whether the result actually works.
